# ADR-0007 — Human Handoff over AI Replacement
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-08-24

## Context
AI can help discovery and reflection but cannot embody pastoral relationship.
## Decision
AI augments navigation, reflection, and trusted-resource discovery and must preserve human handoff. It must not replace pastors, mentors, churches, therapists, or spiritual authority.
## Alternatives Considered
AI spiritual director, counseling replacement, and closed automated companionship were rejected.
## Consequences
Future AI must include `request_human_companionship` and protect sensitive data.
## Implementation Notes
No AI vendor is selected.
