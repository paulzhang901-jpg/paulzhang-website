# ADR-0001 — Platform North Star
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-08-24

## Context
A stable product identity is required before feature development.
## Decision
paulzhang.org is primarily a life-formation + relationship + discipleship + multiplication platform grounded in Truth Library × Life Stories × Growth Companionship. It is not primarily a blog, LMS, personal portfolio, newsletter funnel, or AI chatbot.
## Alternatives Considered
Content archive, course platform, personal-brand site, and acquisition funnel were rejected as primary identities.
## Consequences
Features must enable truth → trust → relationship → formation → community → mission → multiplication.
