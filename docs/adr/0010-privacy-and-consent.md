# ADR-0010 — Privacy and Consent
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-08-24

## Context
Pastoral and formation interactions may include sensitive information.
## Decision
Separate anonymous analytics, functional sessions, communication consent, contact identity, sensitive companionship content, and authenticated data. Consent is explicit and scoped.
## Alternatives Considered
One user metadata blob, inferred email consent, and raw pastoral text in analytics were rejected.
## Consequences
Access, logging, AI use, and future retention need domain-specific controls.
## Implementation Notes
No provider or retention period is selected.

None.
