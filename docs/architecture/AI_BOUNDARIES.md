# AI Boundaries and Human Handoff

AI should function as Navigator, Resource Retriever, Reflection Assistant, Growth Guide, and Human Relationship Bridge. It must not be presented as pastor replacement, mentor replacement, therapist replacement, spiritual authority, or independent theological authority.

Canonical pattern:

Question → understand intent → retrieve trusted canonical content → offer relevant truth → reflection → practice/pathway → human companionship when appropriate.

Every future AI system must preserve a `request_human_companionship` escape path with reasons such as `faith-question`, `grief`, `marriage`, `family`, `life-direction`, `spiritual-growth`, `ministry`, or `other`. Sensitive submissions are not automatically sent to AI.

No AI vendor or retrieval technology is selected. See [ADR-0007](../adr/0007-human-handoff-over-ai-replacement.md) and [Privacy](./PRIVACY_MODEL.md).
