# Product North Star

paulzhang.org is a digital life-formation and discipleship platform grounded in truth, building trust through authentic life stories, enabling learning through trusted resources, fostering growth through relational companionship, and equipping people to accompany others and make disciples.

## Three pillars and progression

**Truth Library × Life Stories × Growth Companionship**

Truth → Trust → Relationship → Formation → Community → Mission → Multiplication

The long-term outcome is Reader → Connected Person → Growing Disciple → Companion → Disciple-Maker.

## Anti-goals

The platform must not become primarily a blog, portfolio, newsletter funnel, LMS, course marketplace, sermon archive, social network, content farm, generic Christian content site, or AI chatbot. Paul Zhang is a trustworthy guide and witness, not the product center.

Do not optimize merely for consumption, clicks, screen time, or email capture. Preserve movement from content into relationship, formation, loving service, and multiplication.

## Governing question

Every substantial feature must answer: **How does this help a person move faithfully from truth into relationship, relationship into formation, and formation into loving service and multiplication?**
