# When Heaven and Earth Are Shaken --- Why Do People Still Hide from the Lamb?

## Introduction: What If Everything You Depend On Can No Longer Hold You?

Brothers and sisters, throughout life we are searching for security.

Some place security in a bank account. If the balance is large enough,
the heart feels calmer.

Some place security in a house. Once the house is purchased and the
mortgage is paid, life seems to have a foundation.

Some place security in physical health. If the medical report looks
good, the future feels manageable.

Others place security in immigration status, work, institutions, a
nation, or technology.

These things are not necessarily bad. We should work responsibly, manage
money, care for our bodies, and take responsibility for our families.

The problem is that we often turn tools God has given us into
foundations that only God can be.

We say, "God is my refuge," while our hearts may actually depend on a
bank balance, property, our children's future, a medical report, a job,
or social stability.

When the sixth seal is opened, John sees a terrifying picture:

the earth shakes;\
the sun becomes black;\
the moon becomes as blood;\
the stars fall;\
the heaven departs;\
mountains and islands move from their places.

Then something even more startling happens.

Kings run.\
Commanders run.\
The rich run.\
The powerful run.\
Ordinary people run.

Everyone is looking for somewhere to hide.

But what frightens them most is not merely the earthquake or the
collapsing heavens. They cry:

> "Fall on us, and hide us from the face of him that sitteth on the
> throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb." (Rev. 6:16, KJV)

The most serious question at the end is not:

"Where can I run?"

It is:

**"How can I face God?"**

## I. Heaven and Earth Are Shaken --- God Exposes Humanity's False Security (6:12--14)

John sees a great earthquake, the sun becoming black, the moon like
blood, stars falling, heaven departing like a scroll, and mountains and
islands moving from their places.

We must read this language carefully.

Revelation does not invite us to match every earthquake, eclipse, or
astronomical event with the sixth seal.

John is drawing on the prophetic language of the Old Testament
concerning the day of the Lord.

Isaiah 13:10 uses darkened sun, moon, and stars in an oracle of judgment
against Babylon. Joel says that the sun will be turned into darkness and
the moon into blood before the great and terrible day of the Lord (Joel
2:31). Haggai speaks of God shaking heaven, earth, sea, dry land, and
the nations (Hag. 2:6--7).

Revelation is not inventing a new vocabulary of disaster. It takes up
the prophets' language to show that when God's judgment reaches its
appointed climax, the created order itself is portrayed as shaken before
the Creator.

The things we regard as most stable---sun, moon, stars, sky, mountains,
earth---are not the ultimate foundation.

### The Purpose of Shaking

Hebrews 12 interprets Haggai's language:

> "Yet once more signifieth the removing of those things that are
> shaken... that those things which cannot be shaken may remain." (Heb.
> 12:27, KJV)

Then:

> "Wherefore we receiving a kingdom which cannot be moved, let us have
> grace." (Heb. 12:28, KJV)

God's shaking does more than produce fear. It exposes false foundations
and reveals what cannot be shaken.

Economies change.\
Housing markets change.\
Positions change.\
Bodies change.\
Institutions change.\
Nations change.\
Technology changes.\
Even people we love will one day leave us.

God is not telling us to stop working, saving, seeing doctors, or taking
responsibility.

He is warning us:

**Do not turn temporary gifts into an eternal savior.**

Revelation 6:12--14 moves from earth to sun, moon, stars, sky,
mountains, and islands. Verse 15 then names people across the social
order. The literary effect is comprehensive: creation is shaken, and
human society is shaken.

### Application: What "Caves" Are We Building?

We may not yet see the climactic shaking pictured by the sixth seal, but
we spend enormous energy constructing caves for ourselves.

A retirement account can become a cave.\
A child's success can become a cave.\
Health can become a cave.\
Education, employment, property, or legal status can become caves.

Even religious activity can become a cave:

"I have attended church for years."\
"I was baptized."\
"I have given money."\
"I have served."

Good things can become ways of avoiding the deeper question of whether
we have truly faced God.

If God allowed the thing you depend on most to be shaken, who would
remain?

If the answer is not Christ, our foundation needs to be rebuilt.

Do not make temporary tools into eternal foundations. Do not put God's
gifts in the place of the God who gives them.

## II. Everyone Hides --- The Most Terrifying Reality Is "the Wrath of the Lamb" (6:15--16)

John lists kings, great men, rich men, chief captains, mighty men,
slaves, and free people hiding among rocks and caves.

No social identity becomes a refuge before God.

Résumé, bank balance, military rank, social class, and public applause
cannot shield anyone from the Creator.

Human societies build hierarchies of wealth, education, influence, and
status. Before God's judgment, however, all stand as creatures
accountable to Him and all need grace.

Then comes one of the most startling expressions in the passage:

**"the wrath of the Lamb."**

A lamb suggests gentleness, sacrifice, and vulnerability. Revelation 5
has just shown us the Lamb "as it had been slain." He sheds His blood
and redeems a people for God.

Yet in Revelation 6, people would rather have mountains fall on them
than face the Lamb.

Why?

Because the Lamb who saves is also the holy and righteous Judge.

### The Cross Reveals Both God's Love and the Seriousness of Sin

We rightly say, "God loves you."

But how deeply does God love?

Christ died for sinners.

> "But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet
> sinners, Christ died for us." (Rom. 5:8, KJV)

The cross also reveals how serious sin is. If sin were trivial, the
cross would not have been necessary.

The cross does not say that God ignores sin.

It says that God takes sin so seriously that the Son bears judgment, and
that God loves sinners so deeply that the Son willingly gives Himself
for them.

That is the gospel.

### Why Is the Wrath of the Lamb So Terrible?

Imagine someone trapped in a burning building. A rescuer risks his life,
enters the fire, reaches out a wounded hand, and says, "Take my hand. I
will bring you out."

But the person refuses---again and again---and rejects the rescuer.

When the fire finally overwhelms the building, the tragedy includes this
fact: rescue came and was rejected.

The analogy is limited, but it helps us feel the force of Revelation's
image. On the day of judgment, humanity does not face a God who never
offered mercy. The Judge is the Lamb who gave Himself and called sinners
to come.

John 3:36 says:

> "He that believeth on the Son hath everlasting life: and he that
> believeth not the Son shall not see life; but the wrath of God abideth
> on him." (KJV)

God does not suddenly change from loving to irrationally angry. He
remains holy, loving, and just.

To reject the Lamb's salvation is finally to face His righteous
judgment.

### Application: Why Do We Hide Instead of Repent?

The astonishing thing is that the people in Revelation 6 recognize the
One on the throne and the Lamb, yet they do not cry, "Forgive us!"

They cry for the mountains to hide them.

Sin is deeper than doing bad things. At its root, sin says:

"I do not want God to be God over me."

People still hide today.

Some hide in busyness.\
Some hide in entertainment.\
Some hide in money.\
Some hide in moral superiority.\
Some even hide in religion.

But true safety is not finding a place where God cannot see us.

True safety is being reconciled to God now.

## III. "Who Shall Be Able to Stand?" --- The True Refuge Is Not a Cave but the Lamb (6:17)

The chapter ends with a question:

> "For the great day of his wrath is come; and who shall be able to
> stand?" (Rev. 6:17, KJV)

Who can stand?

The wealthy? No.\
The powerful? No.\
The morally impressive? Not by their own goodness.\
The religious? Not if religion is merely external.

Revelation 6 deliberately leaves the question hanging.

Chapter 7 answers it.

### Revelation 6 Hides; Revelation 7 Stands

In chapter 6 people hide in caves, collapse in terror, and ask mountains
to cover them.

In Revelation 7:9, John sees a multitude no one can number from every
nation, kindred, people, and tongue:

> "stood before the throne, and before the Lamb, clothed with white
> robes." (KJV)

Notice the verb:

**They stand.**

Chapter 6 asks, "Who shall be able to stand?"

Chapter 7 shows people standing.

Why can they stand?

Revelation 7:14 says that they "have washed their robes, and made them
white in the blood of the Lamb."

That is the answer.

### We Stand Not Because We Are Good Enough, but Because the Lamb Gave Himself for Us

Psalm 130 asks:

> "If thou, LORD, shouldest mark iniquities, O Lord, who shall stand?"
> (Ps. 130:3, KJV)

The answer is: no one.

But the next verse says:

> "But there is forgiveness with thee, that thou mayest be feared." (Ps.
> 130:4, KJV)

The gospel does not say judgment is unimportant.

It says judgment is so serious that the Lamb bore judgment for His
people.

We can stand because Jesus gave Himself for us.

We do not stand on our own righteousness. We stand by the grace of the
Lamb.

### The Main Question Is Not "When?" but "Where Am I?"

Many people read Revelation and ask:

"When will the sixth seal happen?"\
"Which blood moon is this?"\
"Is this earthquake prophetic?"\
"Which political leader belongs to the end times?"

But the final question of the passage is different:

**"Who shall be able to stand?"**

In other words:

When that day comes, where are you?

Hiding from the Lamb?\
Or standing before Him in worship?

Rejecting Him?\
Or belonging to Him?

That is Revelation's urgent question.

## Gospel Call: Today Is Still a Day of Grace

We are reading a vision of future judgment, yet today you can still hear
the gospel.

That means the door of grace is still open.

Today the Lamb's outstretched hand is the hand of salvation.

Do not wait until everything shakes to search for safety.

The true refuge is not a cave.

The true refuge is Christ.

The covering sinners need is not a mountain of rock, but the saving
grace secured by the Lamb's blood.

Lay down pride.\
Lay down self-righteousness.\
Lay down false security.\
Come to Christ.

Confess:

"Lord, I am a sinner. I need Your salvation."

Trust Him. Follow Him. Hide your life in Christ.

Those who belong to Christ can stand when everything else is shaken.

## Conclusion: When Everything Shakes, What Will Remain?

Revelation 6:12--17 gives us three pictures.

First: heaven and earth are shaken. Creation cannot be our eternal
foundation.

Second: humanity hides. Status, wealth, power, and social identity
cannot become the final refuge.

Third: a question rings out---who can stand?

Chapter 7 will answer: those washed by the blood of the Lamb.

So when the world shakes, do not ask only:

"How can I keep my life from ever changing?"

Ask:

"Is my life built upon the Christ who cannot be shaken?"

Wealth can shake.\
Health can shake.\
Homes can shake.\
Institutions can shake.\
The world we know will pass away.

But we have received a kingdom that cannot be moved.

Do not wait for the mountains to collapse before looking for a cave.

Today, hide yourself in Christ.

Do not wait for the wrath of the Lamb to be revealed before remembering
His love.

Today, receive the salvation offered by His nail-scarred hands.

We can stand in judgment not because we are strong, but because the Lamb
gave Himself for us; not because we are without sin, but because His
blood cleanses sinners.

Heaven and earth will be shaken.

Christ will not.

## Response Prayer

Lord Jesus Christ, You are the Lamb who was slain and who rose again.

We confess that we often build our security on things that can be
shaken---money, property, health, status, work, people's opinions, and
even our religious performance---without truly making You the foundation
of our lives.

Forgive us.

Teach us also to know Your holiness. You are the loving Lamb and the
righteous Judge. Your cross reveals the greatness of Your love and the
seriousness of sin.

While the day of grace remains, keep our hearts from becoming hard.

Bring those who have not truly turned to You to repentance and faith.
Lead those who already belong to You away from false securities and
deeper into Christ.

Lord, when everything shakes, cause us to stand---not by our own
righteousness or merit, but by the blood of the Lamb.

May the day come when we no longer hide from Your face, but stand
clothed in white before Your throne with the multitude and cry:

> "Salvation to our God which sitteth upon the throne, and unto the
> Lamb." (Rev. 7:10, KJV)

In the name of Jesus Christ we pray. Amen.
