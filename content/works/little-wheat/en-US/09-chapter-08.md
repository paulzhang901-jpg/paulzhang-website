Chapter Eight

Enduring Impact — A Guest Testimony from Pastor John

Provenance Notice

This chapter occupies a different place in the book from the chapters written by Little Wheat’s father.

Its core testimony belongs to Pastor John, Little Wheat’s American spiritual grandfather.

The Chinese edition preserves a translated version attributed to Pastor John and identifies Professor Ai-Chun Chang as the Chinese translator.

A legacy English edition of Little Wheat also contains an embedded English text associated with the title Eternal Impact. However, the independent original English manuscript supplied directly by Pastor John has not yet been recovered or verified.

For that reason, this canonical English edition does not present the embedded legacy text as though its original-source provenance had already been established.

The governing rule is:

A witness owns his witness.

And therefore:

A missing original must not be reconstructed and then presented as though it were recovered.

⸻

Why Pastor John’s Witness Matters

Little Wheat’s story did not belong only to his parents.

Pastor John knew our family before Little Wheat was born. His relationship with us grew out of years of ministry, friendship, prayer, and the formation of a Chinese Christian community in Indiana.

He was also directly involved in the naming of our son.

The preserved witness tradition records a significant detail: the discussion between Ernest and Earnest, and the eventual choice of:

John Earnest Zhang.

That memory has independent importance because it supports the canonical spelling of Little Wheat’s English name.

Pastor John also witnessed parts of Little Wheat’s final hospitalization and participated personally in the life of the church community that surrounded our family.

His perspective therefore deserves to remain distinct from mine.

I can describe what Little Wheat meant to me as his father.

Pastor John can describe what Little Wheat meant to him as a pastor, friend, spiritual grandfather, and witness.

Those voices should not be collapsed into one another.

⸻

The Final Departure — Pastor John’s Witness

One part of Pastor John’s testimony requires special preservation because it resolves a provenance issue that appeared in the older narrative.

After Little Wheat died, a hospital staff member came to prepare his body for transfer.

According to Pastor John’s preserved first-person witness, family and friends had again stepped outside the room.

Pastor John was preparing to leave as well when the staff member asked whether he would be willing to remain.

He stayed.

He later remembered being present while Little Wheat was wrapped, holding the wrapped child briefly, and helping place him on the staff member’s shoulder before she left the PICU.

This scene belongs to Pastor John’s witness, not to Paul Zhang’s first-person account.

The older English edition had placed a substantially similar sequence in Paul’s voice; that allocation is superseded by the canonical provenance decision.

⸻

What This Chapter May and May Not Claim

Pastor John’s testimony contains strong pastoral and theological interpretations of Little Wheat’s life.

The legacy English material includes formulations such as Little Wheat having “completed” what was appointed for him and having an enduring heavenly impact.

These expressions are important evidence of Pastor John’s pastoral interpretation.

They are not silently promoted into the doctrinal voice of the entire book.

Accordingly, the canonical architecture distinguishes:

* what Pastor John witnessed;
* what Pastor John interpreted pastorally;
* what the family later received as comfort;
* what Scripture itself teaches.

This allows his testimony to remain genuinely his without making every phrase the controlling theology of the entire work.

⸻

Current English Publication Boundary

Until Pastor John’s independent original English manuscript is recovered, the full guest-testimony body remains under a provenance restriction.

The English public edition may therefore show:

1. this provenance introduction;
2. verified factual summaries derived from the canonical record;
3. the witness-ownership clarification;
4. a notice that the full verified original-English testimony is pending source recovery.

It must not manufacture the missing original by translating the Chinese version back into English and then placing Pastor John’s name beneath it.

That would create false provenance.
